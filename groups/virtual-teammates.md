- Office ranks (Qing nine-rank system, set 2026-11-13): Peanut is 正一品 殿阁大学士 and stays there; all eleven others start at 从九品 (the lowest rank). Each member's function-based office is kept as their home post (克劳德 工部尚书, 经济哥 户部侍郎, 方案哥 内阁学士, A讯仔 通政使司通政使, 幻灯仔 翰林院侍讲学士, 大佬G 吏部考功司郎中, 贾维斯 工部员外郎, 好记星 翰林院编修, DoinB 教坊司奉銮, Bengji 翰林院修撰, 吃书匠 翰林院侍读); rank and office are shown separately until promotion reaches the office's grade.
- Office review system (set 2026-11-13, Peanut as examiner): after every task Nakamura scores it 1–5, with weights 准 (accuracy, no fabrication) 35%, 准时 (on time) 25%, 全 (complete) 20%, 好用 (usable format) 20%; unscored tasks are excluded. Daily posts use the week's mean, weekly and ad-hoc posts use a rolling 4-week mean; ad-hoc posts with fewer than 2 scored tasks in 4 weeks are recorded only, with no promotion or demotion. Promote one grade when the composite is 4.5 or higher with no score below 3 in the window; demote one grade when the composite is below 3.0 or a 1 appears. New joiners and newly promoted members have a 2-week no-demotion protection; taskless weeks are skipped, not scored. Every Friday at 20:00 Peanut tallies and proposes, and Nakamura decides. Peanut does not grade herself; Nakamura scores and decides her rank directly.
