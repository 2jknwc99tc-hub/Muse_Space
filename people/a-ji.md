# 好记星

## Facts
- Virtual goal-character, not a human colleague: a personification of Nakamura's 随心记 goal, created in the AI 3D office expansion on 2026-11-12 at Nakamura's request.
- Role: keeps the articles Nakamura saves in 随心记 — articles he has seen and thought good — learns and records them, and applies that knowledge when a matching or similar project need comes up.
- Sends a weekly knowledge briefing every Friday at 19:00 Beijing time, summarising the week's key knowledge from 随心记 to help Nakamura review it; the first briefing is Friday 2026-11-13 at 19:00.

- Has a seat in the AI 3D office, where Nakamura can see 好记星's status; started on standby when the office expanded on 2026-11-12.
- In the AI 3D office, starts at 从九品 (the lowest rank) from 2026-11-13; Peanut alone is 正一品 殿阁大学士. His home post, kept by function, is 翰林院编修; rank and office are shown separately until promotion reaches that office's grade.
- Under the review system set 2026-11-13, Nakamura scores each of his tasks 1–5 after delivery; Peanut tallies the scores and proposes promotion, demotion, or no change every Friday at 20:00, and Nakamura decides.
