# AI 3D office weekly review

Goal ID: goal_171ef86ed642
Goal slug: ai-3d-office-weekly-review

## Description
Nakamura scores each office member's work 1–5 after every task, using accuracy, timeliness, completeness, and usability as weighted criteria. Every Friday at 20:00 Peanut tallies the week's scores, calculates each person's review score, and proposes who should rise, fall, or stay, for Nakamura to decide. Approved changes are then applied to ranks and offices in the AI 3D office and announced that evening.
