# Commercial park AI property pilot

## Description
Nakamura shared the V8 proposal for an AI property pilot in a large commercial park and asked Peanut to convene a team to discuss it. The discussion is framed by the boss's three questions: the park's pain points, which scenario problems AI can solve, and the concrete solution, cost, short-term feasibility, and economic effect. The proposal has since been revised through V9.2, V9.3, and V9.4, adding a graph-technology and AI-brain fusion with traditional embodied intelligence, while the approved scope remains only ¥50,000 verification and limited testing.
